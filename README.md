# ☕ Noir & Bean — Luxury Coffee Experience

> ✨ **Where every cup tells a story.**

**Noir & Bean** هو موقع ويب فاخر لمقهى متخصص في القهوة المختصة، تم تصميمه ليقدم تجربة بصرية راقية تجمع بين الهوية الداكنة، التفاصيل الذهبية، الصور عالية الجودة، والحركات التفاعلية.

المشروع يركز على تقديم تجربة مقهى Premium من خلال واجهة حديثة وسريعة ومتجاوبة مع مختلف أحجام الشاشات.

---

## 🌟 عن المشروع

تم بناء **Noir & Bean** كواجهة رقمية لمقهى فاخر متخصص في حبوب القهوة عالية الجودة والقهوة ذات المصدر الواحد (Single Origin).

يقدم الموقع تجربة متكاملة للزائر تشمل:

- ☕ التعرف على قصة المقهى
- 🌱 استكشاف أنواع القهوة المختارة
- 📋 تصفح قائمة المشروبات
- 🎁 مشاهدة العروض
- 🖼️ استعراض معرض الصور
- ⭐ قراءة تقييمات العملاء
- 🪑 حجز طاولة
- 🛒 إضافة المنتجات إلى السلة
- 📩 التواصل والاشتراك في النشرة البريدية

# ☕ Noir & Bean — Luxury Coffee Experience

> ✨ **Where every cup tells a story.**

**Noir & Bean** is a premium coffee shop website designed to deliver a sophisticated digital experience through a dark luxury aesthetic, elegant typography, gold accents, high-quality imagery, and rich interactive features.

The project combines modern front-end development with a carefully crafted luxury coffee brand experience.

---

## 🌟 About the Project

**Noir & Bean** is a premium specialty coffee website focused on single-origin coffee and an elevated café experience.

The website provides visitors with an immersive experience that includes:

- ☕ Coffee shop story
- 🌱 Featured coffee selections
- 📋 Interactive menu
- 🎁 Offers
- 🖼️ Image gallery
- ⭐ Customer reviews
- 🪑 Table reservation
- 🛒 Add-to-cart interactions
- 📩 Contact and newsletter sections

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 🖤 Luxury Design

The interface uses a premium coffee-inspired visual language featuring:

- Dark backgrounds
- Gold accents
- Elegant typography
- Glassmorphism effects
- Interactive cards
- High-quality photography
- Smooth transitions and animations

---

## 🚀 Loading Screen

A custom loading experience appears while the page is loading.

It includes:

- Animated loading ring
- Coffee bean animation
- **Brewing your experience…** message
- Controlled loading duration

---

## 🧭 Advanced Navigation

The navigation system includes links to:

- About
- Featured
- Menu
- Offers
- Gallery
- Reviews
- Reserve
- Contact

The navigation also includes:

- Sticky behavior
- Active section highlighting
- Mobile navigation
- Backdrop overlay
- Escape-key support

---

## 📱 Responsive Design

The website is designed for:

- 💻 Desktop
- 💻 Laptop
- 📱 Mobile
- 📲 Tablet

The navigation automatically adapts to smaller screens with a dedicated mobile menu.

---

## ✍️ Hero Typing Effect

The Hero heading features an animated typing effect that cycles through multiple phrases:

- Where Darkness Meets Depth.
- The Art of Black Coffee.
- Obsessively Crafted.

The text is typed, paused, deleted, and replaced automatically.

---

## ☕ Featured Coffees

The Featured section presents signature coffee selections such as:

- Yirgacheffe Pour Over
- Harar Natural Espresso
- Guatemalan Cold Brew Reserve

Each card includes:

- Product image
- Description
- Price
- Product status tag
- Add to Cart button

---

## 🛒 Add to Cart

Users can interact with featured coffee products through the **Add to Cart** buttons.

A toast notification displays:

- Product name
- Product price
- Confirmation of the interaction

---

## 📋 Interactive Menu

The menu system provides category tabs:

- 🔥 Hot
- 🧊 Cold
- ✨ Specialty

Users can switch between menu categories without reloading the page.

---

## 🖼️ Gallery Lightbox

The gallery includes a fully interactive Lightbox system supporting:

- Full-size image viewing
- Previous / Next navigation
- Backdrop closing
- Keyboard navigation
- Touch swipe navigation
- Focus management

Supported keyboard controls include:

- Enter
- Space
- Escape
- Arrow Left
- Arrow Right

---

## ⭐ Testimonials Carousel

The Reviews section contains an interactive testimonial carousel with:

- Automatic sliding
- Previous / Next controls
- Navigation dots
- Hover/focus pause behavior
- Interactive navigation

---

## ⏱️ Animated Counters

Statistics and counters animate when their sections become visible, creating a more dynamic browsing experience.

---

## 🎁 Countdown Timers

The website includes countdown functionality for time-sensitive offers and promotional content.

---

## 🪑 Reservation Form

A reservation form allows visitors to enter booking information with client-side validation before submission.

---

## 📩 Newsletter Form

A newsletter subscription form allows visitors to enter their email address and interact with the subscription interface.

---

## ✨ Scroll Reveal Animations

Sections and components reveal themselves as they enter the viewport using the **IntersectionObserver API**.

Supported animation directions include:

- Reveal Up
- Reveal Left
- Reveal Right

---

## 🎨 Ripple Button Effects

Interactive buttons use ripple animations when clicked to enhance the overall user experience.

---

## 🔝 Scroll To Top

A floating Scroll To Top button appears after scrolling down and smoothly returns the user to the top of the page.

---

## 🖼️ Lazy Loading

Images use lazy loading to improve initial page performance and avoid loading images before they are needed.

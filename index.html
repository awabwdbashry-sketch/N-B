Our Story

Obsession
brewed daily

Noir & Bean was born from a single conviction: that coffee deserves the same reverence as wine. In 2019 our founder, Lena Adler, returned from Ethiopia with a suitcase of notes, altitude-grown beans, and an uncompromising palate.

Today we source exclusively from elevation farms — Ethiopia, Yemen, Guatemala — where microclimates forge complexity no roaster can manufacture. Every batch is roasted in-house, on the day it's needed.

Origin First

Direct trade with 12 farms across 4 continents.

Same-Day Roast

Roasted on premises every morning at 5 am.

Zero Compromise

No blends, no shortcuts. Just exceptional coffee.
